Kryton is a self-hosted, markdown-first notebook. It feels like Notion or Obsidian, but the data lives on a machine you control, and any AI agent that speaks MCP can read and write your notes.

This section walks you from zero to a working Kryton with your AI plugged in.

## What's next

- [Install with Docker](/kryton/start/install/docker/) — the recommended path. Two commands.
- [Desktop helper](/kryton/start/install/desktop-helper/) — what to do if you'd rather not touch Docker.
- [Connect your AI](/kryton/start/connect-ai/) — one command wires Kryton into Claude, Cursor, and friends.
- [Your first notes](/kryton/start/first-notes/) — write, link, and explore the graph.

:::tip
Once you're set up, head to [Use](/kryton/use/) for a tour of the editor, search, sharing, and plugins.
:::
