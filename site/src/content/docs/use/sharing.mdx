Kryton notes start out private to you. To bring in a collaborator, share the note explicitly.

## Sharing a note

Open a note → click **Share** in the top right.

### Permissions

- **Read** — the other user can open the note and follow its links, but cannot edit.
- **Read-Write** — full edit access including over the live-collaboration channel; their edits show up in real time and they show up as a presence cursor in your editor.

Folders share too: share a folder, every note inside inherits the permission.

## Access requests

When someone links to a note they do not have access to, they see a "Request access" button. You see the request in **Settings → Access Requests** with Approve / Deny buttons. Approving converts the request into a share with the permission you pick.
