Kryton ships a companion mobile app for iOS and Android.

## Download

- **iOS** — search for *Kryton* on the App Store (store listing pending).
- **Android** — search for *Kryton* on Google Play (store listing pending).

In the meantime, you can install the latest builds from the [Kryton releases page](https://github.com/azrtydxb/kryton/releases) on GitHub.

## Sign in

Open the app, tap **Add server**, and enter the URL of your Kryton — either `https://kryton.ai` or the address of your own self-hosted instance (for example `http://192.168.1.50:3000` on your local network). Sign in with the same email and password you use on the web.

## Online-only

The mobile app talks to your Kryton server live; it does not keep an offline copy of your notes. That means edits sync instantly across devices, but you need a connection to read or write.

Offline support is on the roadmap.
