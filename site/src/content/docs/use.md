Kryton stays out of your way once it's set up. This section is a guided tour of everything you can do once you're in.

## What's in here

- [The editor](/kryton/use/editor/) — markdown, slash commands, tabs, and live preview.
- [Linking and the graph](/kryton/use/linking-and-graph/) — wiki-links, backlinks, and visual navigation.
- [Search and tags](/kryton/use/search-and-tags/) — full-text search and tag-based browsing.
- [Sharing](/kryton/use/sharing/) — invite others to read or edit specific notes.
- [Mobile](/kryton/use/mobile/) — the iOS and Android app.
- [Plugins](/kryton/use/plugins/) — Kanban boards, diagrams, and other extras you can switch on.
- [AI agents](/kryton/use/ai-agents/) — what your Claude, Cursor, or Codex can do once Kryton is wired in.
